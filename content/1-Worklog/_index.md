**On this page**, you will need to introduce your worklog. **How** did you complete it? How many weeks did you take to complete the program? **What** did you do in those weeks?

Typically, and as a standard, a worklog is carried out over about 3 months (throughout the internship period) with weekly contents as follows:

**Week 1:** [Getting familiar with AWS and basic AWS services](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2:** [VPC Peering, EC2 networking and Security Group configuration](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3:** [Transit Gateway multi-VPC connectivity and Hybrid DNS with Route 53](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4:** [Security, monitoring, deployment and cost optimization on AWS](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Microservices, Serverless, CI/CD and AI services on AWS](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6:** [Containerization, Microservices deployment and CI/CD on AWS](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Data Lake and data analytics on AWS](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8:** [AI/ML services and MLOps on AWS](1.8-week8/)

**Week 9:** [TrustBite Review System overview and development environment setup](1.9-week9/)

**Week 10:** [Backend foundation, database design and layered architecture](1.10-week10/)

**Week 11:** [JWT authentication testing, GPS anti-fraud service and CI automation](1.11-week11/)

**Week 12:** [Invoice processing, review APIs, restaurant search and mobile UI completion](1.12-week12/)
